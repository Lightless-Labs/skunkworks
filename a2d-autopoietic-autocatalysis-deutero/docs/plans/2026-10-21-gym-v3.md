# Plan: Gym v3 — a dev gym with headroom (cycle 2 deliverable for cycle 3)

**Created:** 2026-10-21
**Status:** Draft. To be reviewed by Codex Sol and a fresh Claude (copies only) before any authoring.
**Parent:** `docs/plans/2026-10-15-cycle2-nanoharness.md` (addendum 2026-10-21: phase B stops at R = 4).

## Why
The search's ceiling has become the gym's ceiling. On the 140-task dev gym, measured on two full
replicates each:

| Harness | l1 | l2 | l3 | l4 | Families fully solved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E002 | 65/70 | 59/70 | 56/70 | 53/70 | 17 of 35 |
| E007 (incumbent) | 70/70 | 68/70 | 66/70 | 63/70 | 28 of 35 |

With E007 at about 95% all-pass:
- A 35-task phase-B quarter leaves at most about 2 tasks of headroom against a +2-net screen. Rounds 1–3 were all screen-fail (6/6, best +1).
- Any further keep has to come from about 7 tasks in 2 families (cron-next, semver).

A gym that cannot fail the incumbent cannot rank harness changes, nor compare proposers.

## Goals
1. **Difficulty:** the incumbent (E007) passes about 40–60% all-pass (maximum paired power), and D sits well below (about 15–35%), so a D-vs-X claim keeps headroom.
2. **Size:** about 240 tasks (60 families × 4 levels), weighted toward l3/l4.
   - At about 50% pass and about 30% discordance, a single paired replicate has SE ≈ 3.5 pp, about 2.5 pp at r = 2.
   - That detects +10 pp reliably and +5 pp marginally (stated, not hidden).
3. **Coverage of the observed failure classes**, by design rather than by selecting on scores:
   - task-specific requirement misreadings (C2-FA2, C2-FA3);
   - defects that every draft repeats (rational-matrix, rate-sched);
   - algorithmic scaling;
   - multi-stage state machines;
   - larger API surfaces.
   Requirements stay unambiguous (validation and the solve audit enforce it); difficulty comes from depth, not vagueness.
4. **Splits:** dev (search) and dev-holdout (looks), by family, as in cycle 2. A new blind confirmation set is authored separately, later, by the same pipeline.

## Constraints (from confirmation-3, binding until its `decision.json`)
- **`tools/holdout/` is not edited.** The bound runner's drain check runs `c3_author.py --drained` at 00:05 on the spend days, and that code is not byte-bound. Gym v3 tooling is a **copy** under `tools/gym/`, with its own roots (`~/.a2d-gym/`), STOP file, records and driver allowlist.
- **No gym authoring or calibration between 00:05 UTC and replicate completion on 10-24 and 10-25.** It would compete for Sol capacity, which is already voiding tuples.
- Hidden tests never reach a harness or proposer, on dev as well; the oracle's sandbox applies.

## Pipeline
1. **Family briefs** (Sol, high): 80 briefs against a written difficulty rubric (depth, statefulness, scaling, API size), plus a namecheck and a similarity check against every prior task (counts-only against holdouts). Keep 60.
2. **Authoring** (Sol): the c3 sequence, unchanged in substance. SPEC, reference, public and hidden tests, 4 mutants per task; sandboxed jobs; levels l1–l4.
3. **Validation and blind solve audit** (Sol medium, k = 3), as in confirmation-3. An unsolvable task is repaired (requirements only) or dropped.
4. **Calibration by design, then one pilot.**
   - Before any pilot call, fix the selection rule: keep a *family* if its pilot E007 all-pass over its 4 tasks is in [0.1, 0.9]. Families are dropped whole (never single tasks), to limit regression-to-the-mean effects.
   - The pilot is one replicate of D and E007 (k = 5) on every task. **Pilot data are never reused for scoring:** every later experiment and the A/A run use fresh replicates.
5. **A/A on the kept gym** (E007 vs itself, two fresh replicates) sets the keep threshold, as in eval v2.
6. **Freeze:** suite hash, split and threshold are committed; the gym becomes cycle 3's fixed dev set.

## Budget
Remaining Luna after confirmation-3 (≈ 4.5k ring-fenced) and phase-B round 4 is about 3.7k (≈ 5.2k if the relaunch reserve is unused).
- **Pilot:** about 240 × (1.3 D + 3.4 E007) ≈ 1.1–1.5k Luna.
- **A/A:** about 2 × 240 × 3.4 ≈ 1.6k Luna. It may be deferred to cycle 3's own budget if needed.
- **Authoring, audit and briefs:** Sol only (a separate quota).

So, within cycle 2, gym v3 is **built, piloted and frozen**, not searched. Search and the proposer comparison re-run on it are cycle 3.

## Out of scope (cycle 3 design)
- **A tool-using, multi-file regime** (read, write and run in a sandbox; the goal's step-3 alternative). The whole integrity apparatus (allowlist detector, host-off proof, void rules) assumes no tools. A tools regime needs its own integrity model (allowed tool events, sandbox proofs) and its own blind confirmation set.
