## Targets (post-filter, family-clustered)
- **Dev (search) set: at least 180 tasks (about 45 families × 4 levels) after every filter.**
  - Cluster-corrected power, using an inflation of 1.25 (observed in cycle 2) at discordance 0.3:

    | 240 tasks | SE | Power for +5 / +7.5 / +10 pp |
    |---|---|---|
    | r = 1 | 4.4 pp | 0.30 / 0.52 / 0.73 |
    | r = 2 | 3.1 pp | 0.48 / 0.77 / 0.94 |

  - **The minimum useful effect is +7.5 to +10 pp.** +5 pp would need about 900 or more independent pairs, which is stated rather than hidden.
  - At fixed Luna, more tasks beat more replicates.
- **Dev-holdout: at least 25 families, never filtered or piloted on any arm's outcome.** At r = 2 and d = 0.3 the SE is about 4.8 pp, so the +10 pp point bar binds.
- **Difficulty:** E007 (the incumbent) at 35–65% all-pass on the *first fresh* replicate of the kept dev set. D is reported, never targeted. Calibration is on the incumbent only.
- **Regression stratum:** a seeded 15–20% of the E007 4/4 families stays in dev, labelled, so that changes that break easy tasks still show.
- **Authoring volume:** about 85–90 families (dev wave 1 + dev wave 2 + 25 holdout), sized from the measured wave-1 keep rate.

## Frozen rubric (part of the gym SPEC; hashed with the suite; written before any brief)
- **Allowed difficulty sources** (each task tags at least 2; l3/l4 tag at least 3):
  - **depth:** at least 10 numbered requirements at l3/l4, at least 3 of which modify another (precedence or exceptions), each stated affirmatively;
  - **state:** an explicit transition table (at least 5 states at l4), with hidden tests that replay sequences of at least 5 operations;
  - **scale:** an explicit size bound and complexity in the text. Validation shows the reference using **at most 5% of the per-test limit**, and a named `naive` mutant that **exceeds the limit** (so it is at least 20× slower than the reference), under dev parallelism. *(Clarified 2026-10-21: the earlier "20× the limit" was a drafting error.)*
  - **surface:** at least 6 public items with verbatim signatures;
  - **edges:** every boundary a hidden test checks is named in the text.
- **Forbidden:**
  - testing any unwritten convention (ordering, tie-break, rounding, overflow, error precedence, formatting, case, Unicode, float tolerance);
  - unquoted exact error or Debug text;
  - float equality without a stated tolerance;
  - HashMap-order or time-dependent outputs;
  - a literal example contradicted by a hidden test;
  - "trap" wording. An intended misreading is stated as the rule *plus* the rejected reading.
- **Mechanical checks:**
  - **M1 traceability.** Every hidden test carries `// R<n>`; IDs must exist, and every requirement is covered.
  - **M2 mutants.** Each mutant is labelled with the requirement it violates; at least 2 of the 4 are plausible misreadings, each refuted by an explicit sentence.
  - **M3 adjudication.** Two independent Sol adjudicators, in fresh homes. A DETERMINED verdict quotes at least 8 words verbatim from the requirements (checked as a substring) and names the excluded reading. Both must agree; otherwise the test gets a requirements-only repair.
  - **M4 spec review of every task.** The second adjudicator also checks 2 seeded hidden tests per task that the audit *passed*.
  - **M5 opaque names.** Hidden test names are opaque (`h_<hex>`) and appear nowhere in requirements, public tests or the reference. This also removes the coincidental-name hit that voided look 2.
- **Never filter on Sol solvability**, since that favours Sol-judge harnesses. A determined solver failure stays; a Sol failure routes the task to both adjudicators.
- **Class quotas** are fixed before any brief, as equal quotas over a neutral taxonomy. Their origin in the C2-FA analyses is disclosed. Briefs are kept mechanically: the first OK briefs in id order.

## Selection discipline
1. **Seeded family split** (dev / dev-holdout, stratified by rubric class), committed **before any pilot call**. The holdout is never piloted or filtered.
2. **Staged authoring.**
   - Wave 1: about 12 dev families, then an **E007-only pilot** (about 0.2k Luna), then the rubric is adjusted, then wave 2 is authored under the same frozen selection rule.
   - The band rule on the dev pilot: keep a family with **1–3 of 4** E007 passes, plus the regression stratum.
   - Families are dropped whole. There is no replenishment until a favourable pass rate appears, and the attrition floors and stopping rule are registered with the split.
3. **Acceptance check** on the first *fresh* E007 replicate of the kept dev set: 35–65%. Outside that range the *next wave* is adjusted, never a re-filter on that replicate.
4. **No task or family selection uses any replicate that sets the A/A threshold**; if one does, the A/A is re-run.
5. **Pilot only the certified, hashed suite.** A later repair invalidates that family's pilot data. Pilot voids: a void tuple is unknown, and more than 1 void per family means re-piloting that family.
6. **Disclose** that dev deltas can exceed holdout deltas by roughly 1/(kept share) (selection on the incumbent).

## Phase B on gym v3 (cycle 3)
Headroom fixes the ceiling, not the discrimination. On a 60-task quarter at d ≈ 0.3, a null proposal clears +2 net about 36% of the time. The precision half-width stays about 5 pp at R = 6. Re-size the screen and the rounds by simulation before re-running.

### Sizing method (fixed 2026-10-22, before gym v3 exists; numbers are filled in before round 1)
- **Calibration from cycle 2** (`docs/experiments/analysis/c2_noise_calibration.py` → `runs/c2-noise-calibration.json`; dev runs only, aggregates):
  - Same-harness A/A discordance is 0.41–0.57 of the binomial 2π(1−π): v0 0.19 at π 0.75 (10 pairs), E002 0.11 at 0.84 (21 pairs), E007 0.05 at 0.96. Outcomes concentrate per task. At π ≈ 0.5 this predicts d ≈ 0.25.
  - Family-cluster inflation is about 0.96, A/A and candidate vs incumbent alike. The 1.25 used above is conservative.
  - Between-proposal SD of true effects: about 3.5 pp (method of moments over 25 comparisons).
- **Simulator:** `tools/gym/phaseb_sim.py` (stdlib, seeded; `tools/gym/tests/test_phaseb_sim.py`). Placeholder runs: `runs/c2-phaseb-sim/{default,sd02,sd05,many-small}.json`, at π 0.5, d 0.25, 240 dev tasks, 2.25 Luna per task-arm.
- **Findings at the placeholders** (these fix the method, not the numbers):
  1. **Protocol v2's (round, family)-cluster CI is anti-conservative.** The proposals are shared by all tasks of a round, so the between-proposal spread is invisible to it. Its null rejection rate is 0.06–0.19 at a nominal 0.05, growing with round size and spread (0.17–0.19 at 120-task rounds, sd 5 pp). **A round-level one-sided t-test** over the R round differences holds 0.04–0.06 in every cell.
  2. **Power is limited by the number of proposals, not by tasks per round.** For a +5 pp gap at about 0.8 power: 24 tasks × R 40 (0.84, about 6.5k Luna for the rounds), 36 × 30 (0.84, 7.3k), 60 × 20 (0.82, 8.1k). In contrast, 120 × 10 reaches 0.68 at the same 8.1k. A +2.5 pp gap is out of reach (≤ 0.56 at R = 40).
  3. **The keep-rate comparison** (the goal's named endpoint) has power 0.27–0.36 at R = 6 and a +7.5 pp gap (0.46 at most in the sensitivity runs). It reaches 0.8 at about R 15–20 for +7.5 pp, or R 40 for +5 pp. At that size the continuations dominate the cost: a screened proposal pays about 1.9k Luna for its full and confirm stages, so 2 × 40 proposals at a 26–45% null screen rate cost several times the rounds.
  4. **Screens:** protocol v2's fixed +2 net passes 31–40% of null proposals. The A/A-keyed bar passes 4–7%, but keeps only 0.44 at +10 pp on 60 tasks. eval v2.3's thr/2 rule passes 26–44%. The full and confirm stages hold null keeps at ≤ 1.1% under every screen, so the screen trades Luna for power and does not control false keeps.
- **Rule (pre-registered for cycle 3's phase B):**
  1. **Inputs, measured on gym v3 before round 1** and frozen in a LOG entry: π and d from the A/A (the acceptance replicate paired with the first X:inc arm), and Luna per task-arm from the same runs. sd_delta = 3.5 pp (cycle 2), with 5 pp reported as sensitivity.
  2. **Primary self-improvement endpoint:** the round-level one-sided t-test (α = 0.05) on the R differences, X:inf − X:uninf mean all-pass, at a target gap of **+5 pp**. The (round, family)-cluster CI is withdrawn as an inference (it may be shown descriptively, labelled anti-conservative). The sign test stays as a sensitivity check.
  3. **Size:** the cheapest (round size ∈ {24, 36, 60} tasks, R ∈ {20, 30, 40}) with simulated power ≥ 0.8 at sd 3.5 pp, using `phaseb_sim.py` at ≥ 1,500 simulations. If none fits the phase-B budget, run the largest affordable R and state the expected power before round 1.
  4. **Keeps:** the goal's keep-rate comparison is reported descriptively. Continuations use eval v2.3's rules and are capped (at most 1 per arm, as in protocol v2), so they cannot consume the rounds' budget.
  5. **Validity caveat, disclosed:** 20–40 proposals per arm will repeat ideas (cycle 2 repeated by round 4). The test stays valid under the null; repeats are shown with the blinded labels.
- **Review:** the method gets one narrow Codex Sol review (copies only) before it is relied on.
