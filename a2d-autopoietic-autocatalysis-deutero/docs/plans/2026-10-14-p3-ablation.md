# Plan: P3 — Exploratory Ablation and Simplification

**Created:** 2026-10-14 (drafted during P1; runs after the P2 confirmation)
**Status:** Superseded 2026-10-15 by `docs/plans/2026-10-15-p3-research-loop.md` (P2 gate)
**Parent:** `docs/plans/2026-10-14-lift-first-roadmap.md` § P3
**Exit (from parent):** a minimal configuration, recorded as the frozen harness for P4.

## Why

The pilots (E02, E05, E06) show the seed metabolism at k = 5 always runs coder → evolver → architect → tester → coder. In lift mode, two of those four feedback-cycle calls cannot affect the submission as wired today:

- **Architect.** There is no project root, so every `SystemPatch` is rejected (`crates/a2d-cli/src/lift_arm_c.rs`).
- **Tester.** Its `test_results` product is not consumed by the coder. The 2026-10-14 audit already cited this as unconsumed output.

The evolver mutates in-memory enzyme definitions per task, and that may change the second coder prompt. Its effect is unmeasured. Across the pilots, C ≈ D: under the latest-tie rule, C passed 17 and 14 against D's 15 and 16.

## Questions

1. What does each component contribute, holding the invocation cap at k?
2. If inert components are removed and their budget goes to more coder attempts, does C move above D?

## Design

- **Data:** development only, and never claimed. calibration-v2 (24 tasks, 6 families) now serves as development data, plus any new development families. Each comparison is C-variant vs C-seed, paired on the same tasks, at the same k, with D as the anchor.
- **Variants.** Each is a germline edit inside `lift_arm_c`, selected by a variant flag recorded in the registration. Every variant must keep RAF closure (check with `Germline::raf_status`).
  - **C−architect:** drop the architect enzyme. The freed invocation goes to the next scheduled enzyme.
  - **C−tester:** drop the tester.
  - **C−evolver:** drop the evolver. Any `fitness_report` then goes unconsumed; check closure.
  - **C−feedback:** strip `failure_report` from the coder's catalysts. This tests whether A²D's feedback edge matters at all.
  - **C-minimal:** coder plus whichever of evolver or `failure_report` shows a positive estimate, so that k = 5 yields 3 or more code attempts.
- **Measurement:**
  - realised schedule per variant, read from the ledger;
  - hidden pass rate and hidden-pass fraction;
  - tokens.
  The estimates are recorded with paired cluster-bootstrap CIs; with 6 families they are imprecise by design.
- **Rules (roadmap § P3):**
  - Turning a component off by default needs a recorded estimate.
  - Deleting its code needs an upper confidence bound below a meaningful effect, or a significant build-time or complexity cost.
  - Check interactions only for the final minimal configuration against the seed.
- **Budget:** about 24 tasks × (C-variant 5 + C-seed 5) ≈ 240 invocations per variant, so roughly 1,200 for five variants.

## Output

The minimal configuration is frozen at a commit and recorded as the **P4 frozen harness**. The P4 hypotheses (harness self-modification, archive vs ratchet, co-evolved evaluator) are measured against it on Confirmation-2.
