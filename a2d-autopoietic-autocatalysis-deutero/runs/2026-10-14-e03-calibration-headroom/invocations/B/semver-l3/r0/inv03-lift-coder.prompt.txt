Version syntax is MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH[-prerelease][+build]. Split on the FIRST '+' first, then on the FIRST '-' in the part before that '+'. Validate core, then prerelease, then build. Offsets are zero-based UTF-8 byte offsets WITHIN the identifier, and identifier indices are zero based within their section.
