1. **R1–R2:** Deliver one Rust source file in `rust` fences, compiling with edition 2024 and `std` only; no inner attributes/docs. Match every public type, variant, field, derive, and function signature exactly; helpers may be private.

2. **R3–R5, R21:** Precedence starts with raw empty → first raw non-ASCII byte → whole-input cleanup lexical errors → cleanup boundaries → cleaned empty. Discarded comments still undergo ASCII checking, and a later cleanup lexical error beats an earlier boundary or address error. Every offset refers to original bytes; missing cleaned-end positions use `input.len()`.

3. **R22–R23, R29:** Remove outside-protection SPACE, TAB, comments, and valid folds without inserting spaces; adjacent mixed constructs form one maximal run. A fold requires CR LF plus at least one SPACE/TAB and consumes the entire following horizontal-whitespace run. Bare LF, lone CR, and CRLF without that whitespace give `BadFold`; these rules also apply inside comments. Handle at least 20,000 bytes without panics or exponential work.

4. **R24–R25:** Comments allow three simultaneous nesting levels; the fourth opener gives `CommentDepth`. Unclosed comments report the outermost opener; unmatched outside `)` gives `UnexpectedClose`. Ordinary TAB is allowed, CR/LF require folds, other controls/DEL give `CommentChar`; quotes and `@` are ordinary comment content.

5. **R5–R6, R26:** Cleanup protects a quote only when it is the first retained byte, including after leading CFWS. Copy its contents unchanged; backslash skips any following byte without validating it. Later quotes never protect anything. EOF inside protection is not a cleanup error: after boundary checks, closing search gives `UnclosedQuote` at the opener, including for a trailing backslash, before separator checks.

6. **R27–R28:** After all lexical cleanup succeeds, inspect runs in original order. A run is legal only at a cleaned edge or immediately beside a retained `@` on either side; otherwise report its first original byte. This is byte adjacency, not recognition of the eventual separator. Internal atom/domain-dot gaps and closing-quote-to-trailing-text gaps fail; valid CFWS-only input becomes `EmptyInput`.

7. **R7, R21:** Ignore the complete protected quoted prefix when counting separators; every other `@` counts, even inside later quotes. Local validation must finish successfully before any domain validation.

Leading/trailing dots fail at themselves; consecutive dots fail at the second, unless an earlier invalid byte already wins. Preserve local spelling/case and set `quoted: false`.

9. **R10–R12:** A quoted local’s closing quote must end the local; otherwise `TrailingLocal` at the next byte wins before decoding. Finish content validation before checking decoded emptiness/length: empty reports the closing quote, byte 65 reports its origin, and escaped origins are backslashes.
