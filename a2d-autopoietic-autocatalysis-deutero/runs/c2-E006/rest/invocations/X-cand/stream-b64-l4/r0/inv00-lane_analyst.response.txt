1. **R1–R2:** Deliver one std-only Rust 2024 source file inside `rust` fences, with no inner attributes/comments. Match every public signature, field, variant and required trait exactly. Encoder/Decoder have private state fields; they are not unit structs. Default is optional.

2. **R3–R4, R14, R25–R26, R41:** Encode every byte value using MSB-first digits and the configured alphabet. Optional encoding emits padding exactly like Required; only Forbidden omits it. Always zero unused bits. Empty input stays empty; strictness and max_symbols never affect encoding.

3. **R17–R18, R23, R29, R40:** Encoder pushes emit every complete triple and buffer at most two bytes. Insert row separators only immediately before an actual next-row symbol, including a finish tail. A full row alone never emits a separator. Empty open pushes preserve state; successful finish closes permanently.

4. **R5–R8, R16, R26:** After framing and limits, check terminal padding first, then a pending position-two `=`, then illegal padding placement, then alphabet validity. Position-two `=` waits for another `=`; another byte gets BadPadding at that byte. Forbidden rejects every `=` immediately. Completed padded groups reject all subsequent non-skipped bytes.

5. **R9, R25, R35–R36:** Alphabet aliases are never accepted. At normal lookup, opposite-alphabet symbols yield MixedAlphabet only when strict, otherwise InvalidByte—even without an earlier configured-specific symbol. Earlier framing, limit or padding-state checks can override this classification. Other invalid bytes retain their original byte value.

6. **R27–R28, R32:** IgnoreAscii skips exactly 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 32, anywhere, including within or after padding. None skips nothing; other non-ASCII bytes are not whitespace. Validate Wrapped width first in both constructors and one-shot functions: only multiples of four from 4 through 120 are valid, even for empty input.

7. **R29–R32:** Wrapped mode counts padding toward row width and permits short or full final rows. After a full row, further input must begin the exact selected separator; premature separators, other ASCII whitespace, and missing/wrong separators cause BadWrap at the first offending byte. Framing runs even after completed padding and before symbol processing.

8. **R30–R32, R39:** CRLF state must survive chunk boundaries. A wrong byte after expected CR gets BadWrap at that byte; EOF after CR points to CR. EOF after a complete separator points to its first byte. These EOF framing errors precede padding, truncation and canonicality errors; EOF directly after a full row is valid framing.

9. **R37–R39:** Count every non-skipped symbol considered after framing, including `=` and invalid symbols. Before processing a symbol beyond the limit, return Overlong at its physical offset, ahead of all padding/alphabet checks. Exact-limit input still undergoes normal finish checks; Some(0) permits empty or ignored-whitespace input. Never let counters wrap and bypass checks.

10. **R10–R12, R16, R26, R39:** After EOF framing checks, pending position-two `=` gives BadPadding at that `=`. Otherwise Required rejects every nonempty residual group with Truncated at its first symbol. Optional/Forbidden accept residual lengths two or three, reject one there, and accept zero. Scan errors always precede EOF errors; never prevalidate total length.

11. **R13, R33–R34, R39:** Strict checks require the second digit’s low four bits or third digit’s low two bits to be zero for one-/two-byte tails. NonCanonical points to that digit. Check padded tails only upon valid group completion, and unpadded tails only at finish after framing/padding/length checks. Non-strict accepts nonzero unused bits; complete four-digit groups need no such check.

12. **R15, R17, R19–R24, R41:** Decoder pushes emit only completed groups; permitted residual output waits for finish. Track absolute physical offsets across skipped bytes and arbitrary chunks. A failing push returns none of that call’s output; earlier successful outputs remain. Every decoder error is sticky, including finish errors and AlreadyFinished. Padding alone does not close the decoder. All chunkings must yield identical success output or first error.
