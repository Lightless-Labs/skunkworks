1. **R1:** Submit one std-only Rust source file inside `rust` fences, compatible with edition 2024. Implement the exact public enum, variant fields and function, with the required derives; use no inner attributes or inner documentation.

2. **R2, R15–R16:** Preserve record/column order, Unicode, case and whitespace. Do not require equal column counts. Discard quote syntax and decode doubled quotes exactly once; backslashes and comment-like text remain ordinary data.

3. **R3, R10:** Error bytes are absolute, zero-based UTF-8 byte offsets, including any ignored BOM bytes. Error record indices count only previously consumed outside-quote terminators; CRLF counts once, and quoted newlines count zero times.

4. **R4:** Ignore exactly one U+FEFF only at the absolute beginning. BOM-only input is empty; a second BOM or any later BOM is field data. An opening quote immediately after the ignored BOM is legal.

5. **R5–R6:** Empty input produces no records, but `""` produces one empty field. Return a pending record at EOF, including one ending in a delimiter. A terminal newline adds no extra record; each consecutive terminator still contributes its own record.

6. **R6:** Preserve every empty column, including leading and trailing ones: `,` produces two empty fields, and `,,` produces three. A delimiter immediately before a record terminator leaves a final empty field.

7. **R7, R10, R14:** Outside quotes, accept LF and CRLF only; consume CRLF together. Every other outside CR, including EOF CR, is `BareCr` at the CR. Inside quotes, CR requires no following LF.

8. **R8, R11:** A quote opens a field only at its first byte. Whitespace before it makes it `UnexpectedQuote`. After a closing quote, whitespace or another ordinary byte is `TrailingByte`; only comma, LF, valid CRLF or EOF may follow.

9. **R9, R12, R16:** While quoted, consecutive quotes form an escape before either can close the field. A final escaped pair leaves the field unclosed. Report `UnclosedQuote` at the opening quote; a single closing quote at EOF is valid.

10. **R10, R13, R15:** Inside quotes, preserve commas, LF, CRLF and bare CR exactly, without newline normalization. NUL remains forbidden there; every other field character is permitted subject to quote syntax.

11. **R11–R14:** Scan left to right and return only the first lexical error, discarding all accumulated results. Check NUL first at each byte, then outside-quote CR validity before state-specific checks. Thus bare CR after closure is `BareCr`; CR followed by NUL outside quotes fails at the earlier CR. Check unclosed quotes only after scanning all bytes.

12. **R17–R18:** Impose no fixed field-size, field-count or record-count limits. Keep byte/index arithmetic correct throughout supported input sizes, and retain no parsing state between calls, including after errors.
