1. **R1–R2:** Deliver one Rust source file, inside `rust` fences, compiling with `rustc --edition 2024` using only `std`. Preserve the exact public signatures, error variants and fields, and all four required traits; use no inner attributes or inner doc comments.

2. **R3, R9:** The alphabet is exactly `ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZabcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123456789+/`. Decode raw bytes, skip nothing, and report zero-based indices in the original input. Whitespace, non-ASCII bytes, and URL-safe alternatives are invalid.

3. **R3–R4, R14:** Encoding uses most-significant bits first for every possible byte value. Emit four digits per full three-byte block, `==` for a one-byte tail, and `=` for a two-byte tail, with zero unused bits and no newline. Bit arithmetic must avoid overflow for high-valued bytes.

4. **R4, R11, R16:** Empty input succeeds in both directions. At decode EOF, an otherwise incomplete group of one, two, or three alphabet digits is `Truncated` at that group’s first byte, including when complete groups precede it.

5. **R5, R8:** Four alphabet digits emit three bytes and permit another group. Only `xx==` and `xxx=` are valid padded groups, emitting one and two bytes respectively; either makes the decoder terminal.

6. **R6, R16:** Group positions are zero-based. An `=` at position zero or one immediately yields `BadPadding` at that byte; do not defer this to EOF or classify it as an invalid character.

7. **R6–R7, R16:** An `=` at position two is pending, not immediately erroneous. The next byte must be `=`; anything else yields `BadPadding` at that next byte, including whitespace or an otherwise invalid byte.

8. **R8, R16:** After a completed padded group, the very next byte always yields `BadPadding` at its own offset, whether it is an alphabet digit, whitespace, non-ASCII, or another `=`.

9. **R10–R11, R16:** EOF with a pending position-two `=` yields `BadPadding` at that `=`, taking precedence over truncation. Thus `AA=` differs from `AAA`, whose error points to the group’s first byte.

10. **R9, R12, R16:** Apply per-byte precedence: terminal padding, pending padding expectation, illegal `=` placement, then invalid byte. Stop at the earliest detectable scan error; neither length prevalidation nor a separate invalid-character scan may override it or replace it with an EOF error.

11. **R4, R13:** Encoding must produce canonical zero trailing bits, but decoding must accept nonzero unused bits. In particular, `Zh==` returns `f` and `Zm9=` returns `fo`; discard unused bits without validation.

12. **R14–R15:** Successful decoding preserves all reconstructed bytes, including zero and values at least 128. Calls share no state, and any error returns only `Err`, even after earlier groups decoded successfully.
