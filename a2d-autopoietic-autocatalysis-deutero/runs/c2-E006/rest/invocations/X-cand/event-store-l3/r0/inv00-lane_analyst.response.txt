2. **R4–R6, R19:** Apply events in order against pre-event state; every success, including Open/Close and valid duplicates, increments version once. Stop at the first failure with its zero-based index and no partial result. Zero balance never resets version; no implicit events or reopening occur.

3. **R7–R8:** Open checks limit **before status**: only 1 through 1,000,000,000 inclusive is valid. InvalidLimit wins even on Closed. With a valid limit, either Open or Closed yields AlreadyOpened; only New succeeds.

4. **R9–R11:** Deposit precedence is New→NotOpened / Closed→Closed, then nonpositive amount→InvalidAmount, then excess capacity→LimitExceeded. Equality with remaining capacity succeeds. Handle i64::MAX without overflowing; avoid an unchecked balance-plus-amount test.

Floor must lie in 0..=limit even when funds are insufficient; InsufficientFunds beats FloorViolation. Both balance and floor equality succeed; handle i64::MAX safely and never retain floor.

6. **R16–R19:** Close precedence is lifecycle, expected-version comparison, then zero balance. Compare against the version **before** Close; mismatch reports the event’s expected value and current actual value, even with nonzero balance. Success increments version and permanently retains Closed status, limit, and zero balance.
