1. **R1–R2:** Deliver one std-only Rust file in `rust` fences, compatible with edition 2024, without inner attributes or inner documentation comments. Preserve every specified public name, field, variant, signature, and derive; helpers may be private.

2. **R3:** Every `at` is an original-input byte offset. Use `input.len()` for an end position lacking a byte; a removed terminal dot retains its original position when that position supplies an error location.

3. **R4–R5:** Raw `""` alone yields `EmptyInput`. Otherwise, the first non-ASCII byte wins over every syntax error, even inside quotes or parentheses. Never trim whitespace, strip comments, or interpret quotes.

4. **R6–R7:** Count all `@` bytes before validating either part. Zero yields `MissingAt`; two or more yield `MultipleAt` at the second, regardless of earlier invalid characters. With exactly one, an empty local yields `EmptyLocal` at `@`, before domain checks.

5. **R8:** All locals use the dot-atom rules; quoting creates no alternative path. Exactly 64 bytes is allowed; longer yields `LocalTooLong` at byte 65 before inspecting contents. Non-dot characters must match the exact stated ASCII allowlist.

6. **R9:** Scan local contents left-to-right, reporting whichever invalid character or invalid dot occurs first. Leading and trailing dots fail at themselves; consecutive dots fail at the second dot. Successful locals preserve spelling and case, with `quoted: false`.

7. **R10:** Only after local success, remove exactly one final domain dot. Both an absent domain and a domain consisting solely of `"."` yield `EmptyDomain` at the position immediately after `@`. Additional terminal dots remain significant.

8. **R11, R18:** Measure domain length after removing the optional root dot. Exactly 253 bytes is allowed; 254 or more yields `DomainTooLong` at byte 254 before any label error. There is no additional total-address length limit.

9. **R12–R13:** Preserve empty labels and validate labels completely in order. Empty-label positions are the leading dot, second dot of an interior pair, or removed root dot for a final empty label. Exactly 63 label bytes is allowed; longer fails at byte 64 before inspecting that label’s contents.

10. **R14–R15:** Within a label of valid length, check leading hyphen, then trailing hyphen, then disallowed characters left-to-right. Thus a trailing hyphen beats an earlier bad interior character. Only ASCII letters, digits, and hyphens are allowed; brackets and underscores fail.

11. **R16–R17:** Only after every label succeeds, require two labels, reporting `DomainNeedsDot` at domain start. This beats `NumericTld` for a single numeric label. Otherwise, an entirely ASCII-digit final label yields `NumericTld` at its start; mixed alphanumeric or valid internal-hyphen labels pass.

12. **R17–R18:** Success lowercases only the domain and omits its root marker. Apply the complete stated stage precedence even when multiple faults coexist; perform no DNS lookup, IDNA conversion, Unicode processing, or special localhost handling. The three visible tests barely exercise these boundaries and competing errors.
