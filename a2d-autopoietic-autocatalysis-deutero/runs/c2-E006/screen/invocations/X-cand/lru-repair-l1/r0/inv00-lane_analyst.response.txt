1. **R4–R6:** Validate insertion in this exact order: backward time, zero weight, overweight against current capacity, deadline overflow. Return only the first applicable error, including for replacements and zero TTL.

2. **R5, R21:** Every error preserves the entire state, including clock, capacity, accounting, recency, and entries that would expire at the supplied time. Never purge before validation finishes.

3. **R7–R8:** Expiry is inclusive: purge deadlines **<= now**, not merely `< now`. `None` never expires; checked `now + ttl == u64::MAX` is valid, while exceeding it returns `DeadlineOverflow`.

4. **R8–R10:** Every successful timed call purges before its main action and sets clock to `now`, even on misses and no-ops. Purge all expired keys in ascending numeric key order, irrespective of deadlines or recency; `advance` performs nothing further.

5. **R13–R14:** After purging, remove any surviving same-key entry before considering eviction. With `Some(0)`, always stop there: store nothing and evict nothing, even when the purge list is empty. All insertion validations still apply.

6. **R15, R22:** Evict only while the proposed total **exceeds** capacity; equality fits. Avoid overflowing `used + weight`: after weight validation, compare `used > capacity - weight`. A weight of `u64::MAX` fits capacity `u64::MAX`.

7. **R13, R15–R17:** Replacement frees its full old weight before eviction and never reports that removal. Evict oldest surviving keys as needed, then store the replacement as most recent—even when its value and weight are unchanged.

8. **R9, R17–R18:** Returned removal lists concatenate numerically sorted purge keys with capacity victims in LRU eviction order; do not globally sort them. A purged key remains reported even if that same call reinserts it.

9. **R11–R12, R20–R21:** Successful gets touch only the hit key; misses preserve surviving order. Remove preserves other keys’ order. Equal timestamps are accepted, and call order determines recency without ties.

10. **R18–R19:** Resize validates time, purges, installs capacity, then evicts until `used <= capacity`. Zero capacity empties the cache; growing or retaining capacity still purges and advances time while preserving surviving order.

11. **R1–R3:** New caches have clock and used weight zero, including at capacity zero. Getters and snapshots never expire or touch entries; snapshots clone residents in LRU-to-MRU order and retain their stored metadata.

12. **R1, R22–R24:** Support every key/value bit pattern and full-range weights without sentinel collisions, overflow, or documented-input panics. Every removal path must maintain exact used weight and unique snapshot keys; behavior must remain synchronous and deterministic.
