1. **R1–R2:** Submit one std-only Rust source file inside `rust` fences, compilable with `rustc --edition 2024`. Preserve the exact public API, variants and fields; derive `Debug, Clone, PartialEq, Eq` on `SplitError`. No inner attributes or inner doc comments.

2. **R2–R3, R14:** Scan the entire input for NUL before parsing; the first NUL wins over every syntax error, even an earlier trailing escape or unmatched quote. Positions always count original UTF-8 bytes, starting at zero—not characters or output bytes. Environment strings are irrelevant.

3. **R4, R16:** Only ASCII space, tab, CR and LF separate words outside quotes. Unicode whitespace, vertical tab and form feed are literal text. Empty input and separator-only input return an empty vector; repeated separators create no empty words.

4. **R5, R8, R17:** Bare, quoted and escaped fragments concatenate until an actual outside separator. Opening any quote activates a word, even if its content is empty: `''""` produces one empty word, while `'' ''` produces two. Track word activation separately from accumulated text.

5. **R6, R12–R13:** Inside single quotes, only the next single quote closes quoting. Everything else—including backslashes, double quotes, dollars, backticks and newlines—is literal, subject to NUL preflight. A final backslash here yields `UnterminatedSingle` at the opening quote, never `TrailingEscape`.

6. **R7, R13:** Inside double quotes, single quotes and all separators are literal. Only an unescaped double quote closes quoting. An unmatched quote error identifies the currently open delimiter, including when earlier quoted fragments have already closed; it does not identify the word start.

7. **R9:** Outside quotes, backslash consumes exactly the next Unicode scalar and appends it literally unless it is LF. An escaped quote cannot open quoting; an escaped separator cannot split a word. A multibyte scalar must be consumed whole.

8. **R10:** Outside quotes, backslash–LF contributes nothing, activates no word by itself and creates no boundary. It joins surrounding fragments. Backslash–CR instead appends literal CR and activates a word; any following LF remains an outside separator.

9. **R11:** Inside double quotes, remove backslash only before `"`, `\`, `$` or LF. Append the first three literally; consume LF without appending anything. Before every other scalar, retain the backslash and process that scalar normally; this includes CR and single quote.

10. **R12–R14:** After NUL preflight, parse left to right without returning partial results. EOF after a backslash outside quotes or inside double quotes returns `TrailingEscape` at that backslash; inside double quotes this outranks `UnterminatedDouble`. Otherwise EOF in quoting reports its opening delimiter.

11. **R15, R18–R19:** Implement no additional shell syntax. Dollars and backticks never expand or execute, including inside double quotes; wildcard characters never expand. `#`, `;`, `|`, `&`, parentheses, `=`, and tilde are ordinary characters. R5’s mention of “expanded fragments” does not authorize expansion.

12. **R16–R17:** Preserve every appended scalar’s exact UTF-8 representation and word source order. Do not normalize, trim, change case, or discard literal quoted/escaped whitespace. Remove only quote delimiters and escape characters explicitly consumed by these rules; no arbitrary word-length or word-count limit is specified.
